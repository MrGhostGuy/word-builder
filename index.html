How to play
  • Tap a board square to set your cursor (tap again to flip direction), then tap rack tiles — or just drag them — to build your word. Typing letters works too.
  • First word must cover the ★. Premium squares multiply only the turn they're covered. Use all 7 tiles for a +50 BINGO.
  • The ? tile is a blank — it can become any letter, but scores 0.
  • No play? Exchange tiles or Pass. Game ends when someone plays out or after 6 scoreless turns in a row.
